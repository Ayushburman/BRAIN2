## 1. Change Your Physical State (1–3 minutes)

Your brain works better when your body is alert.

* Wash your face with cold water.
* Stand up and walk briskly for 2–5 minutes.
* Take 10–20 deep breaths.
* Stretch your neck, shoulders, and back.
* If you drink caffeine, a moderate amount (such as one cup of coffee or tea) can improve alertness. Avoid excessive amounts.

---

## 2. Ask Detective Questions

Instead of accepting information, train yourself to investigate.

Whenever you see a statement, ask:

* What is the evidence?
* How do we know this is true?
* Could there be another explanation?
* What assumptions are being made?
* What information is missing?
* If this were false, what evidence would I expect?

This habit is the foundation of critical thinking.

---

## 3. Solve a Quick Logic Puzzle (5 minutes)

Before studying or working:

* Sudoku
* Chess puzzles
* Nonograms
* Logic grid puzzles
* Pattern recognition questions

These warm up reasoning in the same way stretching prepares muscles.

---

## 4. Explain Like a Scientist (Feynman Technique)

Take any topic and explain it as if teaching a 10-year-old.

If you get stuck, you've found a gap in your understanding.

Repeat until you can explain it simply.

---

## 5. Train Observation

Look around a room for 30 seconds.

Then close your eyes and answer:

* How many chairs?
* What color was the door?
* Where was the clock?
* Which objects were on the table?

Detectives develop strong observation through deliberate practice.

---

## 6. Play "What If?"

Scientists constantly ask questions such as:

* What if gravity doubled?
* What if this assumption is wrong?
* What if I remove one variable?
* What if the opposite is true?

This builds flexible thinking.

---

## 7. Slow Down Your Thinking

Avoid jumping to conclusions.

Instead:

Observe → Analyze → Hypothesize → Test → Conclude

This mirrors the scientific method.

---

## 8. Practice Mental Math

Spend 5–10 minutes on:

* Percentages
* Multiplication
* Estimation
* Squares and cubes
* Divisibility rules

This strengthens working memory and concentration.

---

## 9. Build Pattern Recognition

Ask yourself:

* Have I seen something similar before?
* Is there a repeated pattern?
* What changes?
* What stays the same?

Experts in many fields rely on recognizing patterns efficiently.

---

## 10. Remove Distractions

To think deeply:

* Silence notifications.
* Keep only one task open.
* Work in focused blocks (for example, 25–50 minutes).

---

# Daily 20-Minute "Brain Activation" Routine

1. 2 minutes: Deep breathing and stretching.
2. 5 minutes: Logic or chess puzzles.
3. 5 minutes: Mental math.
4. 5 minutes: Explain one concept in your own words.
5. 3 minutes: Read a short scientific article and ask, "What evidence supports this?"

---

# Long-Term Habits That Improve Reasoning

Read books that challenge your thinking, such as:

* Thinking, Fast and Slow
* The Demon-Haunted World
* Super Thinking
* The Art of Thinking Clearly
* How to Solve It

Also practice:

* Chess
* Programming
* Mathematics
* Scientific experiments
* Reading research papers
* Writing down your reasoning before checking answers

## The Detective Mindset

Whenever you encounter a claim, mentally ask:

> **Observe → Question → Gather Evidence → Form Hypotheses → Test → Revise → Conclude**

That sequence is shared by detectives, scientists, engineers, and skilled problem-solvers. With repeated practice, it becomes a habit, making careful reasoning feel much more natural.
